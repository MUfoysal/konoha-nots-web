What is Konoha Notes

Seal Your Thoughts

A cinematic note-taking app for shinobi who guard their secrets. Write, organize, and seal your scrolls — beautifully.

📜
Rich Scroll Editor
Write with a full formatting toolbar — bold, headings, lists, code blocks, quotes. Your thoughts, perfectly sealed.
🏷
Tag & Organize
Add tags to every scroll. Filter by pinned or recent. Your archive stays as organized as a Jonin's mission files.
📌
Pin Priority Scrolls
Mark your most critical notes. Pinned scrolls rise to the top — like S-rank missions in a Hokage's briefing.
⚡
Auto-Seal (Autosave)
Every 2 seconds of inactivity, your scroll is automatically sealed. No chakra wasted on manual saves.
🔒
Your Scrolls, Under Your Seal
Save your notes to your account and return to them whenever you sign in. Your private scrolls stay protected behind your shinobi identity.
🌀
Shinobi Profile
Track your writing stats — total scrolls, word count, rank progression from Academy Student to Kage.

History of Konoha

From the first alliance between the Senju and Uchiha clans to the era of peace, the Village Hidden in the Leaves stands as a testament to human will and sacrifice.

Era of Warring States
The Founding Alliance
Hashirama Senju and Madara Uchiha — once bitter enemies — forged the most unlikely of alliances. Their combined vision gave birth to Konohagakure no Sato, the first of the five great hidden villages. The Will of Fire was born here.
Unseal
Third Shinobi World War
The Yellow Flash's Legend
Minato Namikaze's Flying Thunder God technique became so feared that enemy villages ordered their ninja to flee on sight. His sealing of the Nine-Tails into his newborn son Naruto became the defining act that shaped the next generation of Konoha.
Unseal
The Night of Blood
Uchiha Massacre
Itachi Uchiha, ordered by Danzo and the village elders, eradicated his entire clan in a single night — sparing only his younger brother Sasuke. This act, born of duty and impossible love, haunted the village for decades.
Unseal
Era of the Seventh Hokage
Naruto's New Dawn
Naruto Uzumaki, once the village pariah, achieved the impossible — becoming the Seventh Hokage and ushering in an era of peace. His unwavering determination to never abandon a comrade changed the very soul of the shinobi world.
Unseal
